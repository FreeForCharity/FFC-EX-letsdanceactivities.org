Request your Entertainer NOW!

We know you are busy and need to save time so you can schedule your community’s visit online.

Let’s Dance offers service in the entire state of Colorado, scroll through the months to see what time of year we visit your area! You can also sign up for our newsletter so you can get notifications.\

ATTENTION!

TIPS TO UNDERSTAND THIS SYSTEM!

We will confirm our visit within about 24-48 hrs of your submission.

When there is a time/place listed in the calendar that shows a place we are visiting that day, click on it and you will see that address.

We are happy to do two programs a day (one in the morning and one in the afternoon) – so if you see we are in your neighborhood, please feel free to request that day. If its on our way (coming from Boulder) or near you we are thrilled & GRATEFUL to combine trips.

YES THE PRICE IS WHATEVER YOU SAY IT IS!

Please fill out the form and we will email you back with confirmations, invoice, W9, VACCINATION RECORDS and set up information. We are excited to visit you!

PLEASE PLAN TO HAVE YOUR COMMUNITY IN A CIRCLE WITH NAME TAGS WHEN WE VISIT!
